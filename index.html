Frequently asked questions

Is ShotShare free?

Yes. ShotShare is free to use. Your screenshots are stored in your own Dropbox account, so they count toward your Dropbox storage (Dropbox Basic includes 2 GB for free).

Where are my screenshots stored?

In your own Dropbox, inside a dedicated app folder under Apps. ShotShare has no server of its own: images go straight from your browser to Dropbox.

Who can see my screenshots?

Only people you give the link to. Each upload gets a Dropbox shared link that anyone with the link can open. Deleting an image turns its link off.

Do I need to install anything?

No. ShotShare runs in your web browser on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS. Sign in with your Dropbox account and start pasting.

Does ShotShare see my Dropbox password?

No. You sign in on Dropbox's own page using OAuth 2.0 with PKCE. ShotShare only receives a token for its own app folder, and logging out revokes it.

How do I take a screenshot to paste?

On Windows press Win + Shift + S, on a Mac press Control + Shift + Command + 4, and on Linux press Print Screen and copy the capture to the clipboard. Then press Ctrl + V (Command + V on a Mac) on ShotShare.

Which image formats can I upload?

PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, BMP, TIFF, HEIC, AVIF, SVG and ICO, up to 150 MB per file. You can upload several images at once by dragging them in or choosing files.

Can I delete screenshots later?

Yes. Open Your images, tick the screenshots you want to remove and delete them in one go. They are removed from your Dropbox and their links stop working. Dropbox keeps deleted files restorable for a limited time.
